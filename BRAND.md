# SD88ME brand notes

**Line:** Circuit-true instruments for deep states. **Brand line:** Sound made visible.

## Logo
- SD88ME in **Big Shoulders Display 900**, with the **88 drawn as four sand rings** (two stacked rings make an 8; the square of four is the 88).
- Each ring has a dense, rough core with grains sprayed outward, like sand on a vibrating plate.
- Files in `brand/`: `logo.svg` (full detail, large uses), `logo-nav.svg` (light file for menus and footers), `logo-cream.svg` (on dark), `logo-orange.svg` (rings in the accent).
- Always capitals in running text: SD88ME. The handle and plugin folders stay lowercase: `sd88me - VST - Name`.
- Clear space: one ring height on every side. Minimum 90 px wide.

## Mark (the four rings alone)
- Drawn at four sizes: `mark-large.svg` (dense sand, hero and print), `mark-medium.svg` (48–120 px), `mark-small.svg` (bold rings, 24–48 px), `mark-favicon.svg` (16 px).
- `favicon.svg` (orange tile), `app-icon.svg` (ink tile), `stamp.svg` (SD / rings / ME, for avatars and vinyl), `seal.svg` (ring of text, for stickers and installers).

## Type
Big Shoulders Display 900 (headlines, names, logo) · Mona Sans (body) · Martian Mono (labels, specs, catalogue numbers) · Instrument Serif Italic (one phrase per view). All on Google Fonts.

## Motifs
- **Sand plate:** grains settle into the four rings; tap to scatter them into a tone's Chladni pattern.
- **Sigils:** each plugin's Lissajous figure, on cards, in the drifting band and in the ring around the plate.
- **Catalogue numbers:** SD88-001, SD88-002 … like records.
- **Rules and grain:** 1.5 px ink rules, fine paper grain.

## Voice
Exact, warm, lightly mystical. Real numbers in real units, first person, one evocative line per page. Credit upstream work by name.

## The name
Keep SD88ME as brand, handle and manufacturer string. Three readings: S·D·88·ME (initials, birth year, me), *sedate me* (the state), 8 + 8 (the signal). Domain idea: `sd88.me`. A paid line, if needed, is "SD88ME Editions".
