Two finalists

Sand rings and Peaks, in the new greens, blues and oranges

Both now come with a softer lowercase wordmark next to them, so the logo feels less blocky outside the Stamp. The Stamp and Seal stay for avatars, merch and stickers. The small versions use fewer, larger grains or fewer contour lines so they hold up at favicon size.

Sand rings

Four rings of sand: the octave figure seen from above, made by sound. Grainy, soft and alive. Best at medium and large sizes, and on anything printed.

44 · 32 · 22 px

Peaks

Four summits as contour lines: a map of the same figure seen as terrain. Cleaner and sharper at small sizes, and easy to engrave, embroider or print on a plugin panel.

44 · 32 · 22 px
They can work together. Sand rings as the main mark on the site, covers and merch, and Peaks as the small and engraved version (favicon, plugin panel corner, hardware). Both are the same four-lobed figure, so they read as one family: sound shown two ways, as sand and as terrain.

Specimen

Styled like the symbols review page · Instrument Sans, Instrument Serif, Martian Mono · Sand rings

Calm, precise and bordered, like a museum catalogue for instruments. Stone, paper, deep moss green and a touch of orange. The hero splits in two: the words on paper, and on a dark green plate the logo forms itself out of sand. Tap the plate and it scatters into a tone’s pattern, then comes back.

Resonance

Cymatic, softened · Bricolage Grotesque, Instrument Serif · Peaks

Cymatic without the blocks. The heavy condensed caps become a softer, organic grotesque with an italic accent. Thick rules become hairlines and the cards are rounded. Warm sand paper, ink, ocean blue and an orange highlight. The live sand plate stays as the centrepiece.
