Organic symbols

The Quad idea (two 8s seen from above), grown instead of drawn with a compass

Each one is made by a natural process in code: sand settling on a vibrating plate, contour lines over four peaks, a hand-drawn pen line, cells dividing. Each row shows the symbol on light and dark, inside the Stamp (where it replaces the 88), beside the name, inside the Seal, and at favicon size.

Cymatic

Signal’s type and grid · symbol: Sand rings

Signal, warmed up and made organic. Stone-grey paper with grain, ink and one deep blue. The hero is a live Chladni plate: thousands of grains of sand dance until they settle where the plate is still, drawing the pattern of a tone. Tap it to change the note. Sound made visible, which is the brand in one image.

Organic edge
Sand, grain, patterns that form on their own

Nocturne

Observatory’s palette × Signal’s type · symbol: Peaks

Signal’s huge condensed type, set at night in the Observatory’s dusk colours. Behind it, a topographic map drifts slowly, like terrain seen from above or a heat map of sound. Four peaks rise out of it now and then: the symbol, hidden in the landscape. Calm, deep and a little mysterious.

Bloom

Mandala’s warmth × Signal’s type · symbol: Pen, in the Seal

Mandala, grown up. Instead of drawn sigils, slow blurred light moves behind the type like a light leak on film, with heavy grain. The Seal turns slowly like a record label. Soft rounded cards with a colour for each way a plugin is made. Psychedelic and positive, without the festival feel.
