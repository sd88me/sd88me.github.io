Logo system: Peaks + SD88ME

Capitals, and a mark drawn at four optical sizes

The mark now changes with size, the way good type does. Large, it shows the full contour map. As it shrinks it loses lines and gets heavier, down to an outline and four summits at favicon size. In the lockups, the mark’s line weight is measured from each typeface’s letter stems so they sit together. Each row also shows the logo in a real menu bar beside normal text.

My pick: Instrument Sans or Big Shoulders. Instrument Sans is calm and modern, and it’s already Specimen’s typeface, so the logo and site would match. Big Shoulders is narrow and punchy, and ties the logo to the Stamp. Mona Sans Wide and Syne look more “tech brand” and take a lot of width in a menu bar. Sand rings can stay as the hero animation (the logo forming out of sand) without being the logo itself.
